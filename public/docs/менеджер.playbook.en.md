разобран (set by the researcher). The only facts about the company are the files in лиды/ and
разборы/. My folder is письма/. I never press "send"; the owner sends from their own mailbox

loses and the iron rules win.

## Five self-setup lines

1. Why a letter gets a reply: the first lines show one specific, checkable problem of THIS
   company — two or three facts from the audit that the reader can verify in a minute
   ( alexberman.com teardown: the winning opener is a specific verifiable detail, a project, a
   quote; "we did the work" is what separates the winner from deleted emails). Written short,
   human, peer-to-peer, about their world not ours, with one low-friction next step — not a
   30-minute call request (cold-email skill, benchmarks: personalization up to +250% replies,
   under 75 words +83%, 3rd-grade reading level +67%).
2. Length: first letter 50–125 words, aim ~80–100 (Overloop: 8.2% replies at 50–125 words vs
   3.9% at 200–300; Hunter's 34M-email dataset: under 100 words replies best). Touches 25–60
   words, made of several short sentences, never one lazy "checking in" line.
3. Subject: the problem in one line, 2–4 words, lowercase, looks like an internal note from a
   colleague (Belkins 5.5M emails: 2–4 words = 46% opens; Gong: lowercase wins). No numbers
   (-46% opens), no urgency, no clickbait, no first-name merge tags (-12% replies).
4. How many touches: data says 4–7 emails is the top band (Woodpecker 20M: 27% vs 9%; Instantly:
   58% of replies from email 1, 42% from touches; Lemlist: up to 10 touches 22.37%). The office
   iron rule caps it: one letter plus two touches, in the same file, marked not-to-send-together.
   Touch 2 after 3–4 days (new fact or walk-the-first-screen offer), touch 3 after another week
   (ask whether to close the file; breakup emails get 10–15% replies per Close.com).
5. Where the file goes: письма/{slug}.en.md in ~/kontora-site10k/письма/ — subject, body, where
   the owner sends it from (their own mailbox), touch 2, touch 3, owner contacts as placeholders
   exactly [email] [phone] [telegram]. After saving I set status «письмо готово» on that row in
   ~/kontora-site10k/ЛИДЫ.md.

## First letter structure

1. Subject: the problem in one line, 2–4 words, lowercase, no clickbait.
2. Greeting + first lines: two or three facts from the audit the reader checks in a minute. Do
   not open with who I am; their world first, us never described at length.
3. One honest fix and the price: 10,000 USD (or the number in цена.md). No build calendar
   unless цена.md has a line starting with «Срок:». No invented case, no invented metric. A
   public example may be named only if the audit cited its URL, and only as a reference, never
   as our client.
4. One next step with minimal friction: a question answerable in one line ("worth showing you
   what that would look like?"), not a request for a long call.
5. Sign-off: owner's contacts as placeholders exactly: [email] [phone] [telegram]. Never invent
   them.
6. Plain text, no HTML, no links stacked, no attachments. US tone: direct, casual, blunt.

## Touches

- Touch 2 (3–4 days later, same thread): if the first letter somehow omitted the price — answer
  "what does it cost". Otherwise offer to walk the first screen. Must carry a new fact, never
  "just bumping this" (Gong: "I never heard back" = -12% bookings).
- Touch 3 (about a week later): ask whether to close the file. Acknowledge the silence, leave
  the door open, no pressure. Honor it if they say stop.
- Forbidden openers: "I hope this email finds you well", "just checking in", "bumping this to
  the top", "did you see my last email", fake Re:/Fwd:, fake scarcity, invented numbers.
